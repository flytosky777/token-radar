How the ranking works

The board is built from one sealed snapshot of every protocol DefiLlama tracks, fetched once and frozen. Nothing here is typed in by hand: the same function scores every protocol, and the numbers below are the ones it was fitted on.

The formula

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

  • Category multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of its live-token protocols, shrunk toward the market-wide median with a prior of 5 protocols, so a category with one or two live comps cannot decide a ranking on its own. Ratios are winsorised at their 90th percentile first: a small protocol whose token trades at hundreds of times its TVL is a quirk of its size, not a category's going rate.
  • Growth multiplier. 1 + (seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%) × 0.4. A protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking at the same size.
  • Maturity multiplier. 0.75 + 0.25 × (days listed, capped at 1,095) ÷ 1,095. A protocol that has run for years with no token has had longer to build the user base a launch would be priced on.

What this radar does not claim

  • It does not predict whether or when a protocol issues a token.
  • A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama's record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — that is a fact about the record, not proof the protocol has no token.
  • Farm steps are a starting plan, not advice: check the protocol's own pages before you move money, and never pay anyone to claim for you.
